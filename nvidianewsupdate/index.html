Nvidia and AMD News Update

Updated 13 November 2026

Nvidia leadership change

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned on 12 November 2026, amid reports of personal misconduct. He also stepped down from the board, according to Nvidia's announcement as reported by several outlets. Reports cite multiple extramarital affairs, including one with a woman suspected of being a Chinese intelligence asset.

Colette Kress, the company's CFO, has been appointed interim CEO and CFO while Nvidia's board searches for a permanent successor. The board also named Harvey C. Jones as its first chair. No timeline for a permanent CEO has been confirmed.

Nvidia's board has started an independent investigation. The company said it has no reason to believe secrets were compromised. Reports say investors and lawmakers are watching the effect on U.S. export controls and on Nvidia's stability.

Coverage: AP News, BBC, The Guardian, Forbes, Fortune

AMD news

Shares rally. AMD shares rose about 6% (5.86%) on 12 November. Reports say investors sought alternatives in the AI chip industry after Nvidia's leadership turmoil, though one search found no clear evidence the move was caused by the leadership change itself.

6th Gen EPYC processors. AMD made its 6th Gen EPYC server processors (codenamed Venice) generally available on 10 November 2026.

Instinct MI400 GPUs. AMD's Instinct MI400 GPU series is part of its AI accelerator lineup; research indicates the series launched on 23 July 2026.

Sources named in notes: AMD newsroom, TechPowerUp (not re-checked in this update)
