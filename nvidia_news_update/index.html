Nvidia News Update

As of November 13, 2026

Leadership transition

Nvidia is in a leadership transition. CEO Jensen Huang resigned, and Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress is now interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor.

Reported by Nvidia's newsroom, Fortune and The Guardian

Morningstar cuts fair value estimate

Morningstar has lowered its fair value estimate for Nvidia, citing the CEO transition and management turmoil.

Morningstar

Possible new limits on China chip exports

BusinessWorld reports that new U.S. congressional curbs on chip exports to China are being considered that could further restrict China's access to Nvidia chips.

BusinessWorld

Interest in competitors rises

Market interest in Nvidia's competitors, such as AMD, is rising amid the leadership uncertainty.
